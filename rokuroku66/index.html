03 / FIELD NOTES

Isao’s second mechanical analogue replaces long hydraulic leg sections with a chain of short geared vertebrae. Each gimbal bends in two axes. Repeated bends make an arc; increasing bends tighten it into a spiral; both axes together wind a limb around a pole.

Roam links small random decisions: wander, hesitate, feel ahead, gather low, then rush to a new place and settle. Replay a route with its seed, or choose New route. The body rises while sensing and compresses before each burst.

Walk rolls a staggered step through all six limbs. Sensor walk raises the front pair to probe ahead while four limbs carry the body. Green marks planted tips, amber marks a lifted tip, and blue marks a sensor.

Short ivory scales have recessed tails and raised, rounded lips. They retract slightly at a bend, while service loops carry power and data near the spine’s centre.
