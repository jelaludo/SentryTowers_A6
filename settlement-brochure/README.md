# Galactic holiday brochure / Guide look 03

Third standalone candidate at `settlement-brochure/`, linked to the field card and terminal. A cream postcard, pastel landscape, serif headline and a continuous four-stop settlement panorama present the same four-step guide. At narrow widths the instructions become a two-by-two grid and the souvenir footer wraps; no horizontal scroll is intended.

The six machine illustrations reuse the derived wireframes in `../settlement-guide/wireframes/`, with their source GLBs and generator documented there. KORP and MÖRK keep separate positions and different viewing angles. `landscape.svg` and the inline friendly alien are original presentation-only vector art. No model exports or gameplay state changed.

The alien greeting and towel packing buttons are reversible and session-only. They use native buttons, pressed state and an announced greeting. Reduced-motion preferences disable the small hover transition. The Dyson connection remains future story intent: collectors at the star, power received back home; SOL is orbital defence.

Validation: script syntax, greeting and towel toggle states, links/resources and HTTP checks across all three candidates, shared navigation validation and whitespace checks pass. The panorama was rasterized and visually reviewed. Full desktop/mobile browser layout review remains pending: Safari returned `cgWindowNotFound` during this pass.

All three guide candidates are now available for comparison; none has been selected or integrated into the game.
