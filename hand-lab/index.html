01 / PROPORTION

Unequal fingers. A shaped palm.

Graduated finger lengths, a shorter little finger and an offset thumb saddle keep the silhouette close to the concept. The palm has separate thenar, outer and distal pads.

02 / ARTICULATION

Opposition deserves its own axes.

Each finger spreads and bends at three hinges. The thumb has two base axes, an axial rotation and two flexion joints, supported by a two-axis wrist.

03 / ENCLOSURE

Soft surfaces over hard mechanics.

Short ivory shells leave room around the recessed bearings. Exposed service gaps and a ribbed collar suggest protection without claiming a certified seal.
