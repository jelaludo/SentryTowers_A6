Roadmap

This is the ordered delivery plan for the model library and Workshop. It translates game-design goals and the asset collaboration contract into reviewable work. An item moves to completed only when its files, measurements, validation, documentation and hand-off are present; visual or reference-phone review may remain explicitly pending.

Active

  1. HUGIN and Stålheart contract LODs — candidate delivered; review active. Sixteen standalone LOD1/LOD2 files now cover all four damage states and meet the numeric triangle, draw and plain-byte budgets. Required names, transforms, silhouettes and primary animation motion are validated against the detailed sources; optional Meshopt copies are decoded during validation. Stålheart now uses these same canonical tier IDs in its standalone viewer, the HUGIN/Stålheart comparison and the MÖRK fabrication composite; the composite records exact source hashes instead of maintaining a separate reduction. The Workshop visual review is complete. Game-camera, swap-threshold and reference-phone review remain before Reviewed runtime status.
  2. Robotic assembly-line LODs — candidate delivered; review active. Eight standalone LOD1/LOD2 files cover D0–D3 and meet the landmark budgets. D0/D1 LOD1 retain all eight robot rigs through 36 source-matched tracks, omit the unreadable rover workpieces and replace six fragmented conveyor modules with one continuous 24 m belt. Static LOD2 preserves readable robot, gantry, belt and wreck silhouettes. Plain and decoded Meshopt files, sockets, names, damage distinction and rendered views are validated. Game-camera and reference-phone review remain before Reviewed runtime status.
  3. SH02 landing-island distance tier — candidate delivered; review active. The intact deployed rocket and documented 16 × 16 m landing slab are merged into a 2,154-triangle, one-draw static D0 tier. The 21.4 m rocket silhouette, island footprint, cargo socket and source hierarchy lookup nodes are validated in plain and decoded Meshopt files and reviewed from perspective/map cameras. The animated approach asset currently has no D1–D3 states. Map-camera thresholds and reference-phone performance remain pending.
  4. Truthful readiness and naming evidence — candidate delivered; review active. Workshop cards now use the controlled states Original, Legacy derivative, Contract candidate and Reviewed runtime; no collection claims Reviewed runtime without game-side evidence. A deterministic audit inventories all 459 GLBs and 35 manifests, measures export properties, reports legacy debt and enforces canonical filenames plus unique, non-empty, dot-free engine names on all 165 current candidate files. Solar D0–D3 across LOD0/1/2 and Assembly D0–D3 across LOD1/2 received coordinated, published naming migrations. All candidate files and thirteen declared cross-LOD/damage name sets pass; older original/legacy migrations remain explicit follow-up work.

Next contract work

  1. Shared manifest and viewer specification — queued. Adopt one versioned manifest schema for every family: files, tier, damage, role, derivation, hashes, bytes, triangles, draw calls, dimensions, plot, roots, sockets, colliders, clips, materials, textures, selection thresholds, review state and credit. Show the same essentials in every viewer.
  2. Legacy engine-facing naming migrations — queued by family. Enforcement is active and current candidates pass. Migrate only one original/legacy family at a time from the generated audit, publish old-to-new mappings, preserve damage/LOD parity and wait for consuming-game confirmation before retiring pinned aliases.
  3. Game-camera acceptance evidence — queued. Add third-person, optic, map, container and loading-view captures where relevant. Record projected size, swap thresholds and FPS on the reference phone before making performance or game-readiness claims.
  4. Measured optimization pass — queued. Profile units and map-visible environments in representative scenes, then prioritize families by loaded bytes, visible triangles, draw calls, animation cost and repetition. Keep damage states separate from LODs and add distance tiers to units only when their actual staging justifies them.

Experience and world expansion

  1. AFR-01 Seed Foundry / arrival recycling sequence — candidate delivered; review active. The first panel-to-barrel vertical slice reuses the detailed Assembly Line service arm and relabeled barrel structure, supplies an articulated game tier and a one-draw static distance tier, and now composes a separate four-piece SH02 salvage family tier-for-tier. The deployed landing/engine unit, upper tank, top capsule and ISAO emergence module are addressable pieces; salvage progress remains separate from damage and LOD. Game-camera/reference-phone review, successive section-removal states, gameplay resource wiring and sound remain pending. See the arrival recycler concept brief.
  2. SOL-82 orbital laser — original satellite active; game review pending. Original detailed/game/distance exports and four clips are restored unchanged. Review laser-lab cameras, thresholds and reference-phone performance.
  3. SOL-88 Syzygy — separate model delivered; integration/review pending. Three rotating cages enclose a mechanical lens engine. Detailed, articulated game (7,928 triangles / 7 draws / 386,400 plain bytes) and static distance tiers have independent IDs, sources and a viewer. Integrate the six-clip bounded alignment controller when selecting SOL-88; SOL-82 retains its original choreography. Game-camera, release compression, thresholds and phone review remain pending.
  4. Yushi045 Bio-Pearl — first intact candidate delivered; integration/review pending. Three-tier tripod biomass storage for ISAO has a shared live shoulder gauge/sight chamber, optional glass, engine-driven valve and a 25-container instanced preview. Game tier: 1,208 triangles / 2 opaque draws + optional glass; distance: 400 triangles / one draw. Wire inventory and docking, verify the capacity attribute after game release compression, and measure camera thresholds and reference-phone performance.
  5. Mobile fullscreen asset viewing — queued. Make the 3D stage the primary phone experience in portrait and landscape: a true fullscreen control, safe-area-aware layout, collapsible title and controls, thumb-sized inputs, dependable orbit/pinch gestures, reduced render scale where needed and restoration of the previous camera/UI state on exit. Test representative heavy and light viewers on iOS and Android-sized viewports.
  6. Science-grounded futuristic Habitats — discovery queued. Design an original habitat family that projects credible current work in lunar/Martian construction, radiation shielding, closed-loop life support, in-situ resource use and modular logistics into the game’s visual language. Record research provenance and design inference, avoid copied project geometry, and plan detailed, game and static distance tiers plus damage states before production.
  7. Model-linked sound packages — specification queued. Package original or properly licensed WAV masters and runtime OGG derivatives beside appropriate models. Define stable event names for motors, servos, rotors, hover fields, fabrication, impacts, alarms and emotional vocalizations; identify loops and one-shots, loop points, distance curves, loudness targets, attribution and animation cue timing in the manifest. Never embed large audio payloads in GLB files.
  8. Collision and navigation hand-offs — queued. Add simple named collider meshes, walkable/blocked regions, interaction volumes and socket diagrams where gameplay needs them. Keep render geometry out of physics and document engine import settings.
  9. MÖRK / KORP / ISAO style bible — queued. Codify silhouette, armor bevels, graphite/teal/white materials, cyan functional light, amber service markings, mechanical joints, scale cues and controlled exceptions so future vehicles, drones and habitat machinery read as one product lineage.
  10. Progressive and resilient Workshop loading — queued. Load posters or LOD2 first, stream heavier tiers on demand, use content-hashed cache keys, surface loading and failure states, and make the documentation useful when WebGL or remote module delivery is unavailable.
  11. Accessible motion and controls — queued. Respect reduced-motion preferences, provide pause-by-default where appropriate, keyboard-visible controls, non-color status labels, useful text descriptions and captions/transcripts for future sound demonstrations.
  12. Reproducible release packaging — queued. Generate hand-off inventories, SHA-256 hashes, plain/decoded validation reports, derived Meshopt copies, ZIP packages and route checks in one repeatable pipeline. CI must fail on missing files, broken links, schema errors, unstable required names or claimed budgets that do not match measured exports.

Review sequence

Work proceeds in numbered order unless the game developer changes priority. At each item: review the proposed visual and runtime behavior, build the smallest complete vertical slice, validate measured requirements, update the Devlog and Workshop, hand off a commit plus folder, then record game-side feedback before advancing.
