01 / IDENTITY

A different kind of strength.

Ivory struts carry the skeleton. Dense graphite volumes suggest artificial muscles. The source silhouette and worn PBR finish are retained.

02 / SHARED PARTS

A familiar set of tools.

AUREL extremities tie Red Witness to the lineup. Its optical helmet is available separately in Helmet Lab and as a replacement head on AUREL.

03 / FOR DEVELOPERS

See the body in motion.

Ten baked movements on the textured game model: reference pose, crouch, three gaits, jump and four ukemi rolls. Switch to model inspection for the original tiers and joint controls. Muscles remain rigid and digits fixed; gameplay movement and collision need their own integration.
