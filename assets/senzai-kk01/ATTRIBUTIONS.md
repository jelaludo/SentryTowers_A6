## Provenance

Senzai-KK01 upgrades the former LATENT edition using the Senzai-KK02 model and behavior at repository commit `6312e75`. All three source GLBs are geometry-identical derived copies with KK01 filenames and embedded Jelaludo attribution in each GLB asset header. Their engine-facing names, sockets and internal source-family identifiers remain intact. The KK01 runtime is copied from that animation and retains its complete 72-second cycle; the behavior-cue helper and developer-facing viewer are new.

The earlier LATENT model/runtime remain under assets/latent-field/ in the repository. Its original viewer is archived under source/archive/latent-field-v1/viewer/. The current latent-field URL opens Senzai-KK01.
