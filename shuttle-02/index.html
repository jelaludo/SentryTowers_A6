01 / INDUSTRIAL HULL

Built around its engines.

A long armored spine, layered service panels and broad protective cheeks frame the exposed rotary engine mounts.

02 / ROTATING VTOL

From lift to forward thrust.

Both thruster pods turn through 90° on visible trunnions. Tilt them independently or watch the shuttle lift, transition to cruise and return to hover.

03 / SHARED DOCKING

The same way into the station.

The underbelly collar and sliding hatch stay clear at every thruster angle. Roll toward the orbital terminal, capture its east port and open the outer airlock.
