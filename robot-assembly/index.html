01 / THORACIC FRAME

The rib cage sets the volume.

Twelve paired ribs wrap around a curved spine and join a narrow sternum. The lower ribs open toward the waist. Tubes follow the structure rather than hiding it.

02 / OPTIONAL MUSCULATURE

Volume follows the frame.

Charcoal muscle sheaths taper into braided tendons. The layer follows the existing joints and can be hidden or viewed on its own. The original skeleton stays intact.
