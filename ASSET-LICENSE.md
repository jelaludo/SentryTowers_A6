# Model reuse and attribution

You may reuse the original models and animations created for this workshop, including modified versions, provided you give attribution to **jelaludo**.

Include the credit in your project’s credits, asset listing, or accompanying documentation. Suggested credit:

> Models by jelaludo — https://jelaludo.github.io/SentryTowers_A6/

This notice covers original workshop model assets and their animations. Third-party software, dependencies and imported models retain their own licenses.

The imported references under `assets/hand-lab/references/` are excluded from the original-workshop grant above: LiberHand V1 is MIT-licensed, and the LibHand human model is CC BY 3.0. Preserve their included notices and credits when reusing the originals or converted previews. See [Hand Lab reference attributions](assets/hand-lab/references/ATTRIBUTIONS.md) for exact sources, licenses and modifications. AUREL-01 is an original workshop model and remains covered by the grant above.
