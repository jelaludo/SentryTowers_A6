02 / ROUTE STARLIGHTCollector alignment

Each collector can feed one planet receiver. A receiver accepts one beam; extra beams saturate it. Click a collector to rotate to its next reachable world.

The collectors orbit the star. Receivers are on terraformed planets. This is a routing puzzle, not a claim that the current SEED-01 export already reaches stellar orbit.

    Three verbs, one growing system.
