NISOKU–02 / PREHENSILE FOOT

Built to stand.
Free to grasp.

Isao’s mechanism language, adapted for the ground. Ivory shells cover a broad hindfoot; dark tactile pads follow the heel, arch and toe segments. An inward-turning great toe adds a second way to hold on.

01 / PLANTIGRADE

A heel, an arch, a forefoot.

The ankle sits above the hindfoot. A substantial heel and a raised medial arch distinguish this foot from its companion hand. The four lesser toes are deliberately short.

02 / PREHENSILE

One toe opposes the others.

The great toe has a rotating saddle, roll adjustment and two bending joints. On the metal pipe it swings back to meet the four toes from the opposite side.

03 / AGILITY

Movement through the whole foot.

Follow the continuous sequence or isolate heel-to-toe stepping, independent toe ripples, great-toe opposition, balance and rail capture. Pause and scrub to inspect each contact.
