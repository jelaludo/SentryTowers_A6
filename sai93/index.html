01 / HOP & LOCATE

A short flight to the seam.

Four gimballed cold-gas thrusters lift the unit clear. Its legs fold under a longer, narrower ore tank. Flush hull sensors survey the destination from a flat service deck.

02 / LAND & EXTRACT

Plant before drilling.

Shock struts settle the landing. Foot spikes anchor the chassis, then twin counter-rotating augers and three mining lasers work the seam.

03 / RETURN & SERVICE

One complete field cycle.

The unit brings its ore home, extends a screw-transfer tube into the hopper, refills its gas bottles and recharges before the next command.
