01 / LIFTING BODY

One continuous silhouette.

The pointed nose flows into broad swept wings. A narrow dark canopy, recessed engine wells and a fine amber edge keep the hull clean.

02 / VENTRAL AIRLOCK

Dock beneath the cabin.

Two hatch panels slide apart above an extending collar. A 2.5 m clear opening and four capture latches form the external docking mechanism.

03 / ORBITAL TERMINAL

Roll. Align. Capture.

The shuttle rolls 90° to present its belly to the station's existing east-facing port, mates its collar, locks and opens the outer hatch before departing.
