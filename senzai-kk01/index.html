FIELD NOTES / LORE

Potential, given attention.

Senzai is a living intelligence suspended in an immense latent space. Its dark fibers hold possibilities that have not yet become thoughts. Emerald light is the moment a possibility is noticed.

It searches like a field of flowers feeling a breeze. A signal makes many strands agree. When curiosity becomes examination, fifteen tips gather around one small point, listening for what that possibility might become.

Seeds for a developer

Let resources, memories or unfamiliar visitors become signals. Use Focus Lock for a hypothesis, Examining for close study, and Following when curiosity pulls the whole being forward.

Give it confidence, attention limits and a memory of discoveries. These are directions to expand; the included animation provides its physical vocabulary.
